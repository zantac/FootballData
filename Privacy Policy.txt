Privacy Policy for Youth Scores
Effective Date: 13-09-2025
Thank you for using Youth Scores ("the Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.
This Application is designed to be a simple, view-only platform. We want to be completely transparent about the data we do and, more importantly, do not collect.
1. No Personal Information is Collected
We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use Youth Scores.
The Application DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, such as your:
Name
Email Address
Phone Number
Location
Photos or Media
Contacts
Any other personal data
All match results and competition data shown in the app are public information and are not linked to any individual user.
2. Non-Personal Information & Third-Party Services
While we do not collect personal information, our third-party service providers may collect anonymous, non-personal data for the Application to function properly and to improve the service.
3. Children's Privacy
This Application is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal data, we are in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
